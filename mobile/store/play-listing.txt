FULL DESCRIPTION
TABasco brings chords and tabs to your pocket. Search songs, save them to playlists, transpose to your key, and practice along with YouTube or audio — the same experience as tabascomusic.com, tuned for mobile.

• Search a large public catalog plus your library
• Transpose and tools while you play
• YouTube / audio practice player
• Folders & playlists synced with your account
